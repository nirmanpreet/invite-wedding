Live URL: https://invite.nirmanpreet.com — hard-refresh or use a private tab so you are not seeing a cached page.

**Two extra things worth one look each**

- **Print** (share sheet ▸ Print): only the invitation text prints — no dock, no map, no QR sections, header with names/date/venue at the top.
- **Airplane mode after load**: everything you already saw still works except the PDF (it fetches its libraries on demand) — that is expected.

**If a download fails inside an in-app browser** (iOS WhatsApp is the usual suspect): open the link in the system browser instead — Android WhatsApp ▸ ⋯ ▸ **Open in browser**; iPhone ▸ the Safari view's ⋯ ▸ **Open in Safari**.
